# How Bootcamp Connect uses your information

Last updated: [date]

## Who we are
Bootcamp Connect is run by [Jenny Shen / business name]. We are responsible for your information (the "data controller").

Questions or requests: [contact email].

## What we collect
When you sign in with Google, we get your **name, email address, Google account ID and Google profile photo**. We use the photo as your starting profile photo. You can change or remove it at any time.

When you sign up, we save **your track** (Software Developer or Business Developer) and **when you agreed to our terms**.

When you fill in your profile, we save what you enter: **headline, location, about you, links, work experience, skills, projects, what you're looking for and your photo**.

When you sign in, our database provider records **the time, your IP address and your browser** to keep accounts secure.

Please don't put sensitive details in your profile, such as health, religion or political views.

## Why we use it
- To create your account and sign you in. The law lets us do this because it's needed to provide the service you asked for ("contract").
- To save your profile and match you with projects. Also "contract".
- To keep accounts secure. The law lets us do this because we have a good reason and it doesn't harm you ("legitimate interests").

We don't sell your information, show you adverts, or use it to train AI.

## Who can see it
In this early version, **only you can see your profile.** Other members can't. We'll tell you before that changes.

We use these companies to run the service:
- **Supabase** stores your account and profile in London, UK.
- **Google** signs you in.
- **GitHub** hosts the website and may record your IP address for security.

These companies are based outside the UK, so their staff may sometimes access your information from abroad, for example to fix a problem. When they do, the law requires them to protect it to UK standards. [Owner to check the details in Supabase's and Google's data processing agreements.]

## How long we keep it
Until you delete your account. [Accounts with no sign-in for 2 years are deleted: owner to confirm. Only keep this if there's a way to do it, such as a yearly check. Otherwise remove it.]

## Your rights
You can:
- **delete your account** at any time, on your profile page. This deletes your profile straight away
- **see and correct your information** on your profile page
- **ask for a copy** of your information
- **ask us to limit** how we use it, for example while we check a correction
- **get your information in a format** you can take to another service
- **object** to how we use it

For anything you can't do in the app, email [contact email]. We'll reply within one month.

## Information stored on your device
The app keeps you signed in using your browser's storage. It also keeps your posts and messages on your device, because in this early version they aren't sent to our servers. Logging out or deleting your account removes them from the device. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

## Age
You must be 18 or over to use Bootcamp Connect.

## Complaints
If you're unhappy with how we use your information, please contact us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): [ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).

## Changes
We'll update this page when anything changes, and tell you about important changes when you next sign in.
