    How to get your LinkedIn profile as a PDF
    1. Open your profile on linkedin.com on a computer.
    2. Click Resources (or More) under your name and photo.
    3. Choose Save to PDF, then upload that file here.

    LinkedIn doesn't let other apps read your experience and skills directly, so the PDF is the quickest way to bring them over.
